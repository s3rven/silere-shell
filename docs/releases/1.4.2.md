# Silere Shell 1.4.2

Released 2026-10-11.

**Upgrading:** Nothing to do. Notification history still keeps entries until cleared unless
you choose a period under Settings › Feedback › Notifications.

## Added

- Settings search by feature name, row label or configuration key, from the icon beside the
  section heading or Ctrl+F. A result opens its section and highlights the matching row.
- Optional notification history expiry after a day, week or month, with no background polling.
- Drag a notification popup sideways to dismiss it.
- An active VPN and its address show in the Connectivity card and the network widget's hover
  text; click the card row to copy the address.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb for an hour or until morning from its row in the menu.
- Right-click a history entry to remove it, or middle-click it to jump to the app that sent it.
- Edit a custom screen-lock command in Interface settings, with Save, Revert and validation.

## Changed

- Switching menu tabs uses about 10% less CPU.
- The audio visualizer responds to quieter passages instead of sitting nearly flat.
- The centered visualizer draws twice as many bars.
- The visualizer setting says when it is paused by fullscreen or needs the media widget.
- The audio visualizer runs at Eco while on battery and returns to its preset on the charger.
- Colour pickers stop animating while their settings rows are hidden or disabled.
- The notification search field and the calendar month button are rounded to match the
  buttons beside them.
- New icons for the Interface and Spacing settings pages.

## Fixed

### Bar

- Moving diagonally toward an open tray submenu no longer switches to another submenu passed
  on the way.

### Menu and settings

- Hidden colour swatches no longer accept accessibility actions, and selection rings stay
  inside their current options.
- Incomplete command escapes and empty program names are rejected before the lock action is
  enabled.
- Unfinished slider gestures are cancelled when their range or step changes.
- A confirmation button asks again after its time window expires, even when its cleanup timer
  is delayed.
- A failed Bluetooth connection suggests checking that the device is on and nearby before
  forgetting it.

### Notifications

- Updated history entries are kept when a removal uses an older snapshot.
- Notifications from terminal agents no longer show literal `**` around bold words.
- Clicking a notification from an app with several windows, such as kitty, keeps the window
  the app brings forward instead of jumping to another.

### Media

- A short pause no longer restarts the visualizer and its warm-up.

### System

- Stale windows, workspaces and overview state are cleared when niri disconnects.

### Install and updates

- The last successful package update count is kept when its checker exits with an error.
- The installer no longer adds autostart after the final `return` of a Lua exec file, which
  broke the Hyprland config.
