# Silere Shell 1.4.1

Released 2026-10-10.

**Upgrading:** Nothing to do. If `silere doctor` now warns that Quickshell was built for an
older Qt, update Quickshell (0.3.2 supports Qt 6.12) or rebuild it.

## Added

- `silere doctor` warns when Quickshell was built for an older Qt than the one installed,
  a mismatch that is prone to crashing until Quickshell is updated or rebuilt.

## Changed

- The reactive underline's glow fades out more softly at its edges, and its colours follow
  a theme change in one fade.
- Glow rims around the floating bar keep the same thickness at fractional scales such as 125%.
- Wheel gestures share one cleanup timer, and tool error summaries read long logs from the
  end instead of splitting them.
- The forking guide explains how sliders follow a changing device and how to test it.
- On Hyprland the menu, calendar, quick actions and tray menu each map one window instead of
  two, so opening them and switching menu tabs drop fewer frames.
- `dev/check.sh` unplugs a monitor in a nested KWin, when one is installed, and checks that
  popups and Keep Awake move to the remaining screen.

## Fixed

### Bar

- Overlapping notification, network and screenshot glows no longer cut each other short,
  and a critical notification's glow stays red through a screenshot flash.
- The first network disconnect after login flashes the underline, and the network glow no
  longer lingers once motion stops.
- The bar visualizer redraws when the accent colour changes.
- Scrolling the bar's brightness widget changes the level on every notch on backlights with
  few levels.
- The VPN shield appears or clears within seconds of a tunnel coming up or going away,
  including tunnels started outside NetworkManager, instead of up to 15 minutes later.

### Menu and settings

- Volume, microphone, app volume and brightness drags stop when their device changes, so a
  drag can no longer land on the next device. Bluetooth volume writes stop with them.
- Hidden colour sliders and menu mute and expand buttons ignore clicks and scrolling.
- A scroll that starts after a pause no longer carries part of an earlier one.
- The Now Playing header no longer starts with a stray separator when a player gives no name.

### Notifications

- A battery that is already critical when you log in warns once. A low battery at login
  still stays quiet.
- Notifications and the OSD close on time after the system clock jumps back, as on the
  first boot after using Windows.

### System

- When a monitor is unplugged, its notifications, OSD, open menu and Keep Awake move to a
  remaining screen instead of staying hidden. Keep Awake keeps holding off idle meanwhile.
- Night light's estimated sunset no longer moves an hour with summer time when the system
  time zone has no known location.
- With hyprsunset, night light leaves colours neutral during the day instead of slightly warm.
- Tool error messages skip lines that are blank once control characters are removed.
- The troubleshooting guide names the current Settings pages.
