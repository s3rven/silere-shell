# Code of conduct

Silere is a small project, and I'd like it to stay a friendly place to ask questions,
report problems and build things. This applies in issues, pull requests and Discussions.

- Be kind, and assume good faith. A lot of people here are new to Hyprland, niri or
  Linux desktops, and asking is how they learn.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. No insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- No discrimination against anyone, for any reason.
- Keep private information private, yours and other people's.

If someone makes it unpleasant, use Report content in the comment's menu, and I'll deal
with it. I can edit or remove comments, lock threads and block people who keep at it.
